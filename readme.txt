A searchable sermon library with video, audio, transcripts, Scripture, series, speakers, articles, studies and completeness scores.

== Description ==

= Turn every sermon into a resource people can find, explore and use =

Your church puts hours of preparation into every sermon. But once Sunday is over, that teaching often ends up as a video in an archive where very few people ever find it again.

**Seedcast Sermon Library turns your weekly preaching into a permanent, searchable teaching library.**

Every sermon gets its own page where people can watch the video, listen to the audio, read the transcript, explore an article, work through a Bible study, download resources and see the Scriptures connected to the message.

Visitors can explore your teaching by **Scripture, series, speaker and topic**. Someone studying Ephesians can find sermons your church has preached from Ephesians. Someone exploring prayer or marriage can browse messages on that subject. Someone considering a visit can read what your pastors actually teach instead of judging the church from a homepage and a few video thumbnails.

Written sermon content is also available to search engines and AI assistants, helping the teaching your church already produces become discoverable beyond Sunday morning.

Everything below is included in the free plugin. Sermon Library AI is an optional paid add-on described at the end.

= One sermon becomes many ways to engage =

A sermon can include video, audio, transcript, article, Bible study, sermon notes, the questions it answers, Scripture references, speaker and series information, and downloadable PDFs.

Everything stays connected to the original sermon instead of being scattered across separate posts and pages.

A visitor may read instead of watching a 45-minute video. A member who missed Sunday may listen later. Someone studying a passage may find the sermon through Scripture. Someone who arrived with a question may find it answered near the top of the page. A small group may use the Bible study during the week.

= Build a real library, not just a video archive =

**Scripture.** Every book, chapter and passage gets its own page. Overlapping passages are understood intelligently, so a sermon on Ephesians 6:10-20 can be found by someone looking at Ephesians 6:15.

**Topics.** Organize teaching around subjects people actually want to explore, with a limit of three per sermon so a topic page stays a real way in rather than a list of everything.

**Series.** Give every series its own artwork, description, date range and archive.

**Speakers.** Create speaker profiles with a photo, biography, links and every sermon they have preached.

Together, these turn years of Sunday messages into an interconnected body of teaching rather than a long list of recordings.

= Every passage becomes a page worth landing on =

A verse page listing a single sermon is a dead end for whoever found it.

Sermon Library builds each passage page out. The sermons on that passage come first, then the articles written from them, then the rest of your teaching in that book. Someone who arrives looking for Ephesians 6:15 finds the message on that verse and a way into everything else your church has preached from Ephesians.

Books and chapters are real pages too. One address gathers every sermon you have preached from Romans, and another gathers Romans 8, so the connections between years of Sunday messages are something a visitor can follow rather than something only you can see.

Each sermon in the list also shows which passages in that book it actually touches, so a reader can tell at a glance which message is about the verse they came for.

Passages in books your church has barely preached are kept out of search results on their own, and come back on their own once there is enough teaching to make the page worth reading. Nothing to configure, and nothing to remember to undo later.

= Help people discover what your church actually teaches =

Many sermon archives give search engines little more than a title and embedded video.

Sermon Library makes the underlying teaching available as indexable written content and adds structured information about sermons, speakers, audio, video and Scripture. That gives Google, other search engines and AI assistants more context for understanding what your church teaches.

Someone may discover your church while searching for a Bible passage, theological question, topic or sermon article. They did not have to be searching for your church to find something your church taught.

= Keep useful sermon content alive =

Use `[scsl_content]` to display sermon articles and Bible studies anywhere on your site as a list, grid or slider. Each item opens the original sermon directly to that content, so nothing is duplicated at a second URL.

A message preached two years ago can continue helping someone today.

= See how complete every sermon and your entire library are =

Sermon libraries develop gaps. One sermon is missing a transcript. Another has no Scripture reference. Another is missing artwork, notes or written content.

**Every sermon receives a completeness score**, showing how fully its page has been built out and exactly what could be improved.

Sermon Library also calculates an **overall completeness score for your entire library**, so you can see how consistently your church is building useful sermon records across everything you have published.

The Completeness Report shows:

* A score for every sermon
* An overall library score
* Which sermons need attention
* Exactly what is missing from each sermon
* What is missing most often across the library
* Separate sermon-detail and written-content scores
* A week-by-week completeness record

The score uses only the content your church has chosen to publish. If you do not use Bible studies, missing Bible studies will not lower your score.

Instead of wondering where your sermon library needs work, you can see where it stands and what to improve next.

= Already using another sermon plugin? =

Sermon Library imports from Sermon Manager, Sermon Works, Mattytap Sermons and Series Engine.

For supported WordPress plugins, the importer reads sermon data already on your site, shows you what it found and lets you review it before anything is written. Original plugin data is left untouched. It can also identify duplicate speaker names and help you consolidate them during migration.

Series Engine imports from CSV with a review and mapping step before anything is created.

= Turn the recording into content automatically with Sermon Library AI =

Sermon Library gives every sermon a place for a transcript, article, Bible study and the questions it answers. **Sermon Library AI creates this content for you.**

Upload the sermon recording you already made, and Seedcast processes what was actually preached and prepares the transcript, article, Bible study and questions inside the sermon.

Sermon Library AI can also write the short summary that sits at the top of a book or chapter page, describing what that group of sermons covers as a body of teaching rather than repeating any one of them.

Instead of spending hours transcribing, summarizing and writing after every Sunday, your team can begin with the recording you already made, review the generated content and build a more complete sermon record.

= External services =

The plugin itself does not call any external service to work. These are the only cases where a request leaves your site or a visitor's browser, and each one happens because somebody chose it:

* **Spam checking on submitted forms.** If you turn on Google reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha or Cloudflare Turnstile in Settings, each submitted form sends that provider the token their widget produced, along with your site's IP address, so the provider can confirm the sender is not a bot. Nothing is sent when no provider is configured, which is the default. Google: [terms](https://policies.google.com/terms), [privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). hCaptcha: [terms](https://www.hcaptcha.com/terms), [privacy](https://www.hcaptcha.com/privacy). Cloudflare: [terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/website-terms/), [privacy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
* **Embedded video and audio.** A sermon given a YouTube or Vimeo address embeds that player, so a visitor's browser loads it from YouTube (through youtube-nocookie.com) or Vimeo when they open the sermon. No video is embedded unless you give a sermon one. YouTube: [terms](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), [privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Vimeo: [terms](https://vimeo.com/terms), [privacy](https://vimeo.com/privacy).
* **Copying your audio back from Seedcast.** Churches whose sermon audio is hosted on media.seedcast.ai can copy those files into their own media library from Sermon Library > Reclaim Audio. Your site downloads its own files when you press the button; nothing is sent, and nothing happens unless you use that screen. [Terms](https://seedcast.ai/terms/), [privacy](https://seedcast.ai/privacy/).
* **Questions visitors ask, when the assistant is on.** Only where Seedcast AI Engine is installed and the assistant is switched on for your site. A visitor's question, the title of the page they asked from, and the parts of your published sermons and pages found for it are sent through the engine to api.seedcast.ai to be answered. Nothing identifying the visitor is sent, and the questions themselves are kept on your site. [Terms](https://seedcast.ai/terms/), [privacy](https://seedcast.ai/privacy/).
* **Links out to a Bible.** The "Read it" links beside a passage open Bible.com or BibleGateway in a new tab. These are ordinary links: your site sends them nothing, and nothing loads until a visitor clicks.

Sermon Library AI, the optional paid add-on, is a separate plugin with its own readme, and it is the only part that sends your sermon recordings anywhere.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the `seedcast-sermon-library` folder to `/wp-content/plugins/`
2. Activate it through **Plugins**
3. Go to **Sermon Library** in the admin menu

Moving from another plugin? Use **Sermon Library > Import / Export** before deactivating the old plugin so you can compare the two.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How is the completeness score worked out? =

Each sermon is scored on details that make its page useful, including speaker, series, focus passage, recording and image, plus the written sections you have chosen to use.

The library score is the average across every sermon, so it changes whenever you fill in missing content. The Completeness Report lists the exact fields being counted.

= Can I turn completeness scores off? =

Yes. Disable Show completeness scores under Settings > Display. Weekly scores continue recording so the historical record remains available if you turn the feature back on later.

= Can I move sermons from another plugin? =

Yes. Sermon Library imports directly from Sermon Manager, Sermon Works and Mattytap Sermons, and imports Series Engine data from CSV. Existing plugin data is not modified.

= Can visitors download written sermon content? =

Yes. Transcripts, articles, Bible studies and sermon notes each have a PDF download button.

= Can people search sermons by Scripture? =

Yes. Scripture uses a structured passage picker. Visitors can filter by book, and every book, chapter and passage has its own page. Overlapping passages are matched intelligently, and a passage page also shows the articles written from those sermons and the rest of your teaching in that book.

= Does it work with Elementor, Divi, Yoast and Rank Math? =

Yes. Sermon Library is tested with Elementor and Divi. When a dedicated SEO plugin is active, Sermon Library lets it handle standard metadata while still adding sermon-specific structured data.

= Does deactivating the plugin delete anything? =

No. Sermons, series and speakers remain in the database.

== Changelog ==

= 2.76.0 =

* Ask: a visitor asks a question in their own words and gets an answer drawn only from your sermons, their questions, your topic and passage pages and your church details, with links to where it came from. Needs Seedcast AI Engine with the assistant on for your site, invite-only for now. Without it nothing changes.
* Place the ask box wherever you want it with [seedcast_ask], including a page builder header. A setting can also put it in place of your search box, and ordinary search results still appear under every answer.
* Answers are never pages of their own: one address, kept out of search engines and never cached.
* A Questions screen lists what visitors asked, most asked and unanswered first. Add a question and its answer to a sermon or under your topics, ignore it for good, or block whoever asked.
* Topic pages show the questions filed under them, under "Questions people have asked".
* Questions stay on your site for ninety days unless you act on them. Visitors are recorded as a changing hash, never an address.
* Visitors are limited to a few questions a minute, and your site stops itself at a daily limit you set.
* A Topic Coverage screen shows how much of your library carries topics, and can file every unfiled sermon in one run with Seedcast AI Engine.
* Sermon views are counted. They never were: the counter never reached the page. Topic and passage pages are counted too, a cached page still counts, and crawlers do not.
* A service bulletin's opening paragraph is written from its sermon, as the emphasis of the morning, where Seedcast AI Engine is connected.
* A new sermon can start its service bulletin for you. Tick "Create service bulletin automatically" while writing it, and publishing the sermon starts a bulletin for the week it was preached. Needs Bulletin Library.
* A topic page names the subjects it is taught alongside beside its title, under a Related topics heading, with a setting of its own.
* Filtering the sermon list by "Needs work" returns the sermons that need work, and agrees with the Completeness screen.
* The Content column tells the truth about Questions, and the sermon list no longer writes a warning to the error log for each sermon with an empty list.
